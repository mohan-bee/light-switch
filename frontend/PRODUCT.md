# Light

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

React Native, TypeScript, Expo SDK 58, and react-native-svg.

## Product Purpose

Open directly to the rocker switch for the selected GO Switch ESP32 device.

## Operating Context

The mobile app discovers saved switches on Wi-Fi and reads the selected relay's current state. Device settings handle pairing over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi changes. The web preview toggles a local rocker.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Toggle the rocker with a tap, click, or Space on web.
- Store paired device credentials in the phone's secure storage and use the selected device's confirmed relay state.
- Receive authenticated live relay updates and rediscover the device when the connection is lost.
- Expose the checked state to assistive technology and respect the system reduced-motion preference.

## Brand Commitments

Use the established white canvas, dark ink, and restrained blue accent. Keep the single centered SVG rocker and its localized blue LED glow.
